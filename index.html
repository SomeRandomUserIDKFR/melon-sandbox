    Sandbox controls

    • Spawn livings, props, machines, weapons — then experiment.
    • MP — LAN multiplayer. First joiner is host; share the Network URL. Dragged/selected objects glow each player’s color. Host resyncs the scene automatically when things change.
    • Right-click or double-click a living for orders: Walk, Fight, Flee, Follow, or Stop — plus Activate / Freeze / Kill.
    • Activate turns machines, guns, bombs, and vehicles on/off (off by default).
    • Rope / Soft / Weld / Pin / Signal — soft links, rigid welds, wall pins, and activation wires.
    • Grip — click near a hand and/or item to make the living hold it (snaps into the palm). Click a held item or occupied hand to drop. Guns fire when Activated.
    • Slice — clean limb cuts. Pliers — cut ropes, welds, pins, signal wires, pipes, and grips. Platform — drag to place map editor slabs.
    • Squeezer (Activate) — feed skinned limbs/bodies/corpses into the top; juice yield scales with part size (empty full corpse ≈ 50%, full juice ≈ 150%). Leaves bones behind; skeleton parts don’t count.
    • Graft Vat (Activate) — pipe juice into its tank, then feed skeleton parts into the top. Spends its own juice to restore skin tinted by that juice (pumpkin juice → pumpkin flesh; mixed juices → blended flesh) and top up the living’s juice.
    • Bone Melter — melts skeleton parts into bone melt (pipeable). Metal skeletons (robots / metal frames) yield metal bone melt instead — forges ×3 HP metal frames. Metal Melter (Activate) — melts Metal Rods, Metal Plates, and robot / metal skeleton limbs into liquid metal. Liquid metal works in Limb Regrow / Body Grow / Bone Repair / molds to forge metal bones for any slot. Sword / Spike / Axe / Club Molds cast bone weapons when filled + Activated. Bone Join realigns and reconnects scattered bones into a standing skeleton. Bone Repair (Activate) — pipe bone melt in, then feed injured parts (works under flesh): mends broken bones / sprains and reseats lost joints. Metal frames need metal bone melt or liquid metal; organic parts accept either (metal melt upgrades the frame). Flesh parts take 2× longer and 1.5× more melt than bare bone.
    • Limb Regrow (Activate) — two tanks: pipe bone melt / metal bone melt (left) and juice (right). Grows missing limbs one-by-one in a standing pose (like the Regrow syringe) on the machine bed. Metal melt forges steel bones (×3 HP); juice coats them in any flesh (pumpkin juice → pumpkin flesh on metal arms). Juice alone can also re-skin skeleton parts.
    • Body Grow (Activate) — same dual tanks as Limb Regrow, but forms a new living from the head outward on the bed. Only one body at a time; next starts after the current one finishes.
    • Sensor / Button / Toggle + Signal wire = activation circuits.
    • Clothes — touch a living (or Equip) for hat, helmet, vest, cloak.
    • Save / Load stores the scene (download + browser autosave).
    • Maps: Lab / Yard / Warehouse / Highway / Void / Bone Lab / Acid Pool.
    • Juice fills each living (shown on the torso). Below ~20% they go KO (unconscious).
    • Heal revives without restoring juice — they stay up until juice drops again. Regen / Regrow restore juice (and flesh).
    • Pipe transfers liquid source → dest. Works with livings, syringes, juice Barrels, and empty Tanks.
    • Juice loop — juices mix by color. Crystallizer → shards → Shard Smelter (required) → crystal melt. Molds: bone melt = ivory; crystal/hybrid only from smelted shards (tint / hybrid bone).
    • Pierce weapons (sword, axe, spike) — tip-first stabs bleed juice for a few seconds.
    • Poke / Gun / Boom for impacts. Juice spills, skeletons remain.
    • Fire / Electricity / Water / Acid — Torch, Firecan, Lava ignite; Battery, Baton, Tesla, Shockpad shock (wet = worse); Water pools extinguish + buoy + drown; Acid pools dissolve flesh (same as Acid syringe); Sprinkler puts out fires.
    • Space pause · R clear · scroll zoom · drag empty space to pan
