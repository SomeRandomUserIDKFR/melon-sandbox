Sandbox controls

  • Spawn livings, props, machines, weapons — then experiment.
  • Freeze locks objects. Rope links two bodies.
  • Juice fills each living (shown on the torso). Below ~20% they go KO (unconscious).
  • Heal revives without restoring juice — they stay up until juice drops again. Regen / Regrow restore juice (and flesh).
  • Pipe transfers liquid source → dest. Works with livings, syringes, juice Barrels, and empty Tanks.
  • Syringes — Acid melts flesh; Heal / Regen / Regrow as above. Regen/Regrow on a severed limb forks a new living.
  • Poke / Gun / Boom for impacts. Juice spills, skeletons remain.
  • Fire / Electricity / Water — Torch, Firecan, Lava ignite; Battery, Baton, Tesla, Shockpad shock (wet = worse); Water pools extinguish + buoy + drown; Sprinkler puts out fires.
  • Space pause · R clear · scroll zoom · Alt-drag pan
